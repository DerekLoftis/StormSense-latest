StormSense Latest Forecasts and Sensor Observations

Updated every 6 hours; updated 2026-10-31 18:20 UTC. Links maintain the same address and are overwritten upon each update at about 00:20, 06:20, 12:20 and 18:20 UTC.

Disclaimer: StormSense™ Predictive Plots are computer-generated operational storm tide forecasts; the user assumes any and all risk.

Observations come straight from each gauge's operator: NOAA CO-OPS, USGS, the NWS National Water Prediction Service, the VIMS THREDDS server, FloodVISION and GreenStream. The astronomical tide is StormSense's own harmonic tide for each gauge, locked to the 30-day mean water level (m30, the teal line on the 72-hour forecasts). The forecast adds to that tide the storm surge of the guidance with the best recent skill at the station and lead time (see the skill page), starting from the residual the gauge shows now; each chart names its guidance below the disclaimer. The shaded cone is the 10%–90% range of StormSense's own past errors at each lead time.

NAVD88 charts show the NWS flood categories (Action, Minor, Moderate, Major) with the chance of reaching each within 72 hours in parentheses, and the MLLW line; MLLW charts show the tidal datums. Forecast high waters within 0.5 ft of Action stage (NAVD88 charts) or above HAT (MLLW charts) are labelled XHW. Click a line's label to hide or show it.
